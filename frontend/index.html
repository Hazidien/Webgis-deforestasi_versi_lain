REMOTE SENSING · WEBGIS

WebGIS Deforestation

by Hazidien Ramadhan Utomo

This WebGIS is provided to simplify remote-sensing analysis without requiring users to write Google Earth Engine code directly. It provides an interactive map for exploring forest extent and change within a selected Area of Interest (AOI).

1Define the Area of Interest (AOI) by drawing a polygon or rectangle on the map, or upload a Shapefile ZIP.
2Select the analysis method you want to run. Only the selected module will be executed.
3Click the analysis button to process the AOI and view the resulting statistics, interactive map overlay, layer controls, and classification legend.

Deforestation module

The Deforestation module follows the supplied Landsat/GEE workflow for forest screening across benchmark years 1990–2020. Because a large AOI at 30 m resolution can produce a very large raster, the GeoTIFF download may be unavailable when the requested image exceeds Earth Engine download limits. In that situation, the interactive map and PDF report remain the appropriate ways to inspect and document the analysis.

NDFI Change Detection

NDFI Change is intended for a relatively focused study area. Avoid very large AOIs such as an entire province. For practical processing, use an AOI within approximately a city or regency and compare two observation dates within a one-year analysis window. Larger areas can exceed Earth Engine processing-memory limits, especially because the workflow uses Landsat 8 median compositing and SMA/NDFI calculations at 30 m resolution.

Important: The results are remote-sensing screening indicators, not certified land-cover classifications. Cloud, shadow, seasonality, drought, harvest, mixed-pixel and AOI-edge effects should be reviewed before formal reporting.
